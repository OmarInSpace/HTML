Manchester United

Manchester United Football Club—popularly nicknamed The Red Devils—is one of the most successful, historic, and widely supported professional soccer teams in global sports history. Based at the iconic Old Trafford stadium in Greater Manchester, England, the club competes at the highest level in the English Premier League.

Arsenal

Arsenal Football Club—universally known as The Gunners—is a cornerstone of English football, representing one of the most culturally significant and historically decorated institutions in world sport. Based in Holloway, North London, the club is globally revered for its commitment to progressive, fluid attacking football, an elite youth academy system, and a pioneering role in the modern commercialization of the game.

Liverpool

Liverpool Football Club—universally recognized by their nickname The Reds—is one of the most historic, trophy-laden, and widely supported powerhouses in global football history. Operating out of their world-famous stadium, Anfield, the club competes at the absolute pinnacle of the English Premier League.
